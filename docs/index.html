Habilitate — Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026 · Last updated 15 October 2026

Habilitate is a self-tracking app for people in recovery from alcohol or drug use. It counts sober days and provides a short in-app game to get through a craving. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your information.

Habilitate is not treatment and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose anything and cannot tell whether you are safe.

This policy is governed by the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

2. The short version

  • The app works fully without an account. You are never required to give a name, an email address or a phone number.
  • By default, everything you record stays on your own phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • There is no advertising in the app, and no analytics or tracking software of any kind.
  • We never sell your information, and we never share it with employers, insurers, medical schemes or advertisers.
  • You can erase everything at any time from Settings, in one step.

3. What the app records

Always, on your device only

  • An anonymous identifier generated at random when you first open the app. It is not linked to your phone, your SIM, your name or any advertising ID.
  • The date you entered as the start of your current run, your total sober-day count, and the dates on which you recorded that you used.
  • For each session of the in-app game: the date and time you opened it, how long you lasted, how many moves you made and your reaction times within the game.
  • Whether you have unlocked the paid features.

This information is held in your phone's app storage. It is removed when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Delete everything.

Be aware: this information is stored on your device without additional encryption by the app. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app and see your records. If that is a risk for you, use your phone's own screen lock, and consider not using an account.

Only if you choose to create an account

Creating an account is optional and exists for one purpose: so that your sober-day count survives a lost, stolen or reset phone. If you create one, we process:

  • Email and password sign-in: your email address and an encrypted form of your password.
  • Google sign-in: your email address, your name and your profile picture, as supplied by Google. Habilitate will also appear in the list of connected applications in your Google account.
  • Your recovery records described above, so they can be restored to a new device.

You can sign out at any time. Signing out does not delete anything stored on your phone.

Only if you buy a subscription

Purchases are handled by the Google Play Store and by RevenueCat, which tells the app whether your subscription is active. We never see or hold your card number or bank details. If you use the hardship option to unlock the paid features at no cost, we do not record a reason, ask for proof, or treat your account differently in any other way.

What the app never collects

  • Your location.
  • Your contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • Your calls or messages. The helpline numbers in the app open your own phone dialler; we are not told whether you called, or who you called.
  • Any advertising identifier, and any form of cross-app or cross-site tracking.

We process it only with your express consent, given by choosing to use the app and record that information. You may withdraw that consent at any time by deleting your data and uninstalling the app.

5. Who else sees it

Nobody, unless you choose one of the following:

  • Supabase — the service that stores your backup, if you create an account.
  • Google — if you choose Google sign-in, Google knows you signed in to Habilitate.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat — if you buy a subscription.
  • Anyone you send your summary to — the counsellor report is generated on your phone and shared only when you choose to send it, to whoever you choose to send it to. We are not sent a copy.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, for example under a court order.

Cross-border transfer. If you create an account, the servers holding your backup, and the payment and sign-in services above, may be located outside South Africa. Section 72 of POPIA permits this where you have consented or where the recipient is bound by comparable protections. Creating an account is your consent to that transfer. If you do not want your information to leave the country, do not create an account — the app works fully without one.

6. How long it is kept

  • Information on your phone is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Backup information is kept while your account exists, and deleted within 30 days of you deleting your account.
  • Records of purchases are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • Ask what information we hold about you, and get a copy of it (section 23).
  • Ask us to correct or delete information that is wrong, misleading, excessive or out of date (section 24).
  • Object to our processing of your information (section 11(3)).
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Complain to the Information Regulator.

Most of this you can do yourself and immediately: Settings → Delete everything erases every record on the device. For anything else, email dt3bza@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Children

Habilitate is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, as section 19 of POPIA requires. Account information is sent over encrypted connections, and passwords are never stored in a readable form. As stated in section 3 above, records held on your own phone are not separately encrypted by the app, so your phone's screen lock is an important part of keeping them private.

If a breach occurs that is likely to affect you, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as section 22 requires.

If we change how the app handles your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.
